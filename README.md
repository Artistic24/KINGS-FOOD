# Kings Food Marketplace

I want to create an app for a company called St Kingston it's a company divided into many sectors like fowl farming, chicken and cheese, buggers, pizza fries, men and female Hairstyles, fashion dress and shoes super market. Now in the app you will create an online store and a very smart payment and very detailed GPS location delivery system in Cameroon's 10 regions .
